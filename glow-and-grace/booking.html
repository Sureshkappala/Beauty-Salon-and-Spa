Helpful Answers

Frequently Asked Reservation Inquiries

Everything you need to know about preparing for and managing your appointment.

How far in advance should I reserve my appointment?

For weekday appointments, 1 to 2 weeks advance booking is recommended. For coveted weekend slots and bridal party packages, we suggest booking 3 to 6 weeks early.

Can I book multiple treatments on the same visit?

Yes. Our concierge can coordinate back-to-back or combined services—such as a stone massage followed by a facial and blow-dry—into a seamless retreat day.

How do you accommodate severe skin allergies or sensitivities?

Please note any known allergies in the special requests box during booking. We will review your profile and offer a complimentary 15-minute patch test 24 hours prior.

Can I redeem gift vouchers and membership points online?

Yes. Simply enter your gift voucher code or member ID in the booking notes, or present your digital card to the front desk concierge upon arrival.
