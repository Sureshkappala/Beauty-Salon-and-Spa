Skin Wisdom

Skincare Ritual FAQs & Post-Facial Care

Essential guidance to maximize the longevity of your radiant post-facial glow.

How should I prepare my skin before a facial ritual?

Pause retinoids, glycolic acids, and physical scrubs 48 hours beforehand. Arriving without makeup is helpful, though our therapist will perform a double botanical oil cleanse upon arrival.

Can I apply makeup immediately after my facial?

We advise giving your skin a 12-hour rest to absorb active botanical peptides and hyaluronic acids. If attending an event, we apply an organic mineral tinted SPF with a featherlight finish.

What interval is best for professional facial treatments?

A monthly visit synchronizes perfectly with your skin's natural 28-day cellular turnover cycle. For bridal prep or hyperpigmentation correction, bi-weekly rituals are ideal.

Are these therapies safe for sensitive rosacea-prone skin?

Yes. We have specialized calming protocols using cold rose quartz globes, blue chamomile, and colloidal oatmeal that soothe inflammation without heat or aggressive friction.
