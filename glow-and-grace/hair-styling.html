Expert Guidance

Frequently Asked Hair & Styling Questions

Practical advice from our Master Stylists on color longevity, hair wellness, and appointment preparation.

How do I preserve balayage vibrancy between visits?

We recommend sulfate-free botanical cleansers, lukewarm water rinses, and our weekly organic toning gloss mask. Avoid high heat tools above 350°F without thermal protective serum.

Are your hair coloring formulations ammonia-free?

Yes. We use premium low-odor, ammonia-free botanical dyes infused with argan and camellia oils that deliver multi-tonal dimensional shine without stinging or chemical fumes.

How frequently should I schedule a precision haircut?

For structured bobs and bangs, every 6 to 8 weeks preserves edge crispness. For long layered styles, a botanical dusting every 10 to 12 weeks prevents split-end migration.

Should I wash my hair right before a styling appointment?

For updos and color sessions, hair washed 24 hours prior provides optimal natural grip and scalp oil protection. For haircuts and treatments, we provide an indulgent wash upon arrival.
